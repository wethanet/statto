# Next 4 Weeks Training Plan

## Coaching Intent

This block is designed for an inexperienced or mixed-skill group. The aim is not to add more concepts. The aim is to repeat a small set of decisions until players can act earlier, simpler, and with less panic.

Current focus:
- Handball decisions are improving, but execution still needs calmer, cleaner reps.
- Players are still learning whether they should attack the ball or become the outlet.
- Defensive kicks can become rushed and too straight back toward goal.

Main messages:
- Get the ball first.
- If you are not getting the ball, become an outlet.
- Make a triangle around the ball carrier.
- Play the way you face to earn time.
- Use quick handballs off the ground as one movement.
- Exit defence wide before going long and straight.
- When kicking, choose the best option: goal, contest, or lateral pass to a better player.

Shared language:
- "Ball" means win it.
- "Outlet" means get outside the contest and talk.
- "Triangle" means one player at the ball, one behind or sideways, one ahead or wider.
- "Face" means use the option your body already sees.
- "Wide first" means exit defence toward the boundary-side option, not straight back to goals.
- "Mark, breathe, scan" means kick to a mark, slow down for one beat, then look lateral before going long.

Session shape:
- 10 min warm-up and touch.
- 12 min simple technique.
- 12 min decision drill.
- 15 min pressure drill.
- 20-25 min conditioned gameplay.
- 6 min reset, questions, and recap.

If a drill breaks down, reduce pressure first. Do not add more instructions. Make the area bigger, remove a defender, or walk through the decision at half speed.

Every session finishes with gameplay. For now the gameplay should usually be one of these:
- Changing Lanes: kick to a mark, stop, scan, then pass laterally to change lane before going forward.
- 5v5 Transition: win the ball, find an outlet, kick to a mark, slow down, look lateral, then attack.

## Week 1 - Ball Or Outlet

Theme: Decide earlier whether to get the ball or become the outlet.

Goal: Players learn one simple choice: if you are closest, get the ball; if you are not closest, get out and talk.

Key cues:
- Closest player says "ball".
- Next player says "outlet".
- Outlet gets sideways, not behind the ball winner's back.
- Kick to a mark, breathe, scan lateral.

### Main Session

1. Warm-up: Pairs Touch And Talk
   - Time: 10 min
   - Setup: Pairs handball and short kick 5-10 m apart.
   - Rule: Every receive gets a call: "ball", "outlet", or the receiver's name.
   - Coach: Keep it relaxed. This is just touch and voice.

2. Technique: Ball Or Outlet Pairs
   - Time: 15 min
   - Setup: Pairs start 5 m apart. Coach rolls ball between them. One player attacks the ball, the other becomes outlet.
   - Rule: The player closest to the ball must call "ball". The other must call "outlet".
   - Coach: Reward the correct role and early talk before worrying about perfect execution.
   - Easier: Walk the first two reps.
   - Harder: Roll the ball slightly to one side so the outlet has to move.

3. Decision Drill: 2v0 To 2v1 Outlet
   - Time: 15 min
   - Setup: Start with 2 attackers and no defender. After 5 minutes, add one passive defender.
   - Rule: The ball winner must use the outlet. The outlet then kicks to a marked target.
   - Coach: If both attackers go to the ball, stop and repeat. Do not add extra rules.
   - Easier: Defender shadows only.
   - Harder: Defender can pressure after the ball is picked up.

4. Gameplay: Kick-Mark-Lateral
   - Time: 25 min
   - Setup: 5v5 if numbers allow, otherwise 4v4 or 6v6. Use a rectangle with three lanes.
   - Rules:
     - A team scores by kicking to a mark.
     - After a mark, the player must stop, breathe, and look lateral.
     - The next pass should change lane if a lateral option is open.
   - Coach: This is not about fast scoring. It is about slowing the panic after a mark.
   - Easier: The mark cannot be tackled.
   - Harder: Add a 4-second scan clock after the mark.

5. Review
   - Time: 10 min
   - Questions:
     - Who decides to get the ball?
     - If you are not that player, where do you go?
     - After a mark, what do we do before going long?

### Second Session Option

Repeat Ball Or Outlet Pairs, then spend most of the session in Kick-Mark-Lateral. Keep the coaching to two cues only: "ball or outlet" and "mark, breathe, scan".

## Week 2 - One-Move Ground Handball

Theme: Handle ground balls quickly without turning one action into three actions.

Goal: Players learn to tap, scoop, or handball off the ground in one movement when that is the best option.

Key cues:
- One move, not three.
- Low body, eyes up.
- If you cannot pick it up cleanly, move it to space.
- Play the way you face.

### Main Session

1. Warm-up: Low Hands Relay
   - Time: 10 min
   - Setup: Lines of 4. Players roll, gather, and handball to the next player.
   - Rule: Stay low through the handball.
   - Coach: Smooth movement matters more than speed at first.

2. Technique: One-Move Handball
   - Time: 12 min
   - Setup: Pairs. One player rolls a ball slightly short. Receiver handballs while staying low.
   - Progression: Roll to left and right side so players use the way they face.
   - Coach: Stop players who pick up, stand up, then handball. Reset the body position.

3. Decision Drill: Pick Up Or Punch Out
   - Time: 12 min
   - Setup: 2 attackers, 1 defender. Coach rolls ball into space.
   - Rule: Ball winner chooses either clean pickup or quick ground handball to outlet.
   - Coach: The right decision is based on pressure. No pressure means collect. Pressure means move it.

4. Pressure Drill: 4v3 Ground Ball Exit
   - Time: 15 min
   - Setup: 4 attackers, 3 defenders, 15 m by 15 m grid with one safe outlet zone.
   - Rule: First possession must be below knee height: pickup, tap, or ground handball.
   - Scoring: 1 point for first touch, 1 point for outlet, 1 point for exit.
   - Easier: Defenders start 2 m away.
   - Harder: Defenders start shoulder to shoulder.

5. Gameplay: 5v5 Transition To Mark
   - Time: 20 min
   - Setup: 5v5 if numbers allow, otherwise 4v4 or 6v6. Coach starts each play with a ground ball.
   - Rules:
     - First possession should be one movement where possible.
     - Team must find an outlet, then kick to a mark.
     - After the mark, the player slows down and looks lateral before going forward.
   - Coach: Freeze only when the first decision is unclear. Ask, "Was that pickup or outlet?"

6. Review
   - Time: 6 min
   - Questions:
     - When should we pick the ball up?
     - When should we move it off the ground?
     - What does "one move" look like?

### Second Session Option

Use the 5v4 handball transfer drill as the main block if numbers allow. Finish with 5v5 Transition To Mark so the handball work connects to game play.

## Week 3 - Wide Exit From Defence

Theme: Remove panic from defensive exits by giving players one clear first look.

Goal: Players look wide before kicking straight back toward goal.

Key cues:
- Wide first.
- Boundary-side outlet.
- Play the way you face.
- Kick to space or a contest wide, not panic straight.
- If the best player is lateral and open, use her.

### Main Session

1. Warm-up: Wide Lane Kicking
   - Time: 10 min
   - Setup: Three lanes: middle, left wide, right wide. Players kick and follow to wide lanes.
   - Rule: Every kick starts with a look to the wide lane.
   - Coach: Players should turn their shoulders before kicking.

2. Technique: Mark, Turn, Wide
   - Time: 12 min
   - Setup: Player marks or receives handball in defensive 50. One wide option presents.
   - Rule: Receiver must turn wide before kicking.
   - Easier: No pressure.
   - Harder: Add a passive mark or closing defender.

3. Decision Drill: Backline Three Options
   - Time: 12 min
   - Setup: Ball carrier in defence. Three targets: wide safe option, straight contest, lateral open player.
   - Rule: Ball carrier must call the option before kicking.
   - Coach: The best kick is not always the longest kick.
   - Scoring: 2 points for correct option, 1 point for execution.

4. Pressure Drill: Defensive Exit Gate
   - Time: 15 min
   - Setup: 5 attackers exiting defence against 4 defenders. Two wide gates and one central gate.
   - Rule: Wide gate is worth 2 points. Central gate is worth 1 point only if it is clearly open.
   - Coach: Do not ban the middle. Teach them to recognise when it is safe.

5. Gameplay: Changing Lanes
   - Time: 20 min
   - Setup: Half-ground game split into three lanes.
   - Rules:
     - From defence, first look is wide.
     - A marked player must slow down and look lateral.
     - Team gets a bonus point if it changes lane before going forward.
   - Coach: Praise calm sideways kicks. They are buying time.

6. Review
   - Time: 6 min
   - Questions:
     - What is our first look from defence?
     - When is straight okay?
     - What does a good wide option do before the kick?

### Second Session Option

Run Backline Three Options and Changing Lanes again, then finish with unpressured kicking pairs. Low-skill players need clean reps after pressure reps.

## Week 4 - Connect The Whole Chain

Theme: Win it, shape up, play the way we face, and exit wide.

Goal: Players connect the previous three weeks in game-like situations without needing lots of coach instruction.

Key cues:
- Ball or outlet.
- Triangle.
- One move.
- Face.
- Wide first.
- Best option.

### Main Session

1. Warm-up: Three-Cue Touch
   - Time: 10 min
   - Setup: Groups of 4. Coach calls one cue each minute: "triangle", "one move", "wide".
   - Rule: Players must change their movement to match the cue.
   - Coach: Keep this light and upbeat.

2. Technique: One-Move To Wide Kick
   - Time: 12 min
   - Setup: Ground ball to outlet, outlet kicks wide to target.
   - Rule: Ball must move from ground to outlet to wide kick in three seconds.
   - Easier: Remove time limit.
   - Harder: Add a closing defender.

3. Decision Drill: Goal, Contest, Or Lateral
   - Time: 12 min
   - Setup: Ball carrier 35 m from goal. Three options present: shot, contest, lateral open teammate.
   - Rule: Ball carrier must choose the best option, not the default option.
   - Coach: Ask after each rep, "Why that option?"
   - Scoring: 2 points for best choice, 1 point for execution.

4. Pressure Drill: Contest To Wide Rebound
   - Time: 15 min
   - Setup: Coach starts with a ground ball. Team must win it, use triangle support, then exit wide.
   - Rule: Turnover if all supporting players run to the ball.
   - Coach: This is the main test of the month.

5. Gameplay: 5v5 Transition Or Changing Lanes
   - Time: 20 min
   - Setup: 5v5 transition if numbers are tight, or Changing Lanes if you have enough space.
   - Rules:
     - Around contest, one player must be outlet.
     - First kick after winning it should be to a mark where possible.
     - After a mark, slow down and look lateral.
     - From defence, use width before going straight.
   - Scoring:
     - 1 point for normal score.
     - 1 bonus point for ball or outlet being clear.
     - 1 bonus point for a lateral pass after a mark.
     - 1 bonus point for a wide defensive exit.

6. Review
   - Time: 6 min
   - Questions:
     - What improved most from week 1?
     - What cue helped under pressure?
     - What is the one thing we take into game day?

### Second Session Option

Run a shorter 5v5 transition game with the same rules. Keep stoppages short. Let leaders or captains call "mark, breathe, scan" after each mark.

## Measurement

Track only a few things so it stays simple:
- How often does one player clearly call "ball" and another becomes outlet?
- How often does a triangle exist within 3 seconds of a contest?
- How often does the first defensive exit look wide?
- How often does a pressured ground ball become a one-move disposal?
- After a mark, how often does the player slow down and scan lateral?
- In match sim, how many kicks go to the best option rather than the first option?

Use numbers if helpful, but do not turn training into admin. A simple coach tally during the final game is enough.

## Coaching Notes

Keep corrections short:
- "Who is ball?"
- "Where is outlet?"
- "Show triangle."
- "One move."
- "Play face."
- "Wide first."
- "Mark, breathe, scan."
- "Best option."

For low-skill players:
- Give them more space.
- Let them start as the outlet before asking them to win the ball.
- Praise the decision even when the skill misses.
- Repeat the same drill shape before adding a new one.

For stronger players:
- Add time limits.
- Add a closing defender.
- Make them use their non-preferred side.
- Ask them to lead the talk and organise the triangle.
